ABOUT DIAMOND MEDIA GROUP

Creating Stories That Travel The World

Diamond Media Group is an Australian independent media company bringing together broadcasting, television, film production, travel media and digital publishing.

Who We Are

Diamond Media Group develops and produces premium media content across multiple platforms including radio, television, streaming media, feature films and destination marketing. Our brands connect audiences with travel experiences, tourism organisations, entertainment and lifestyle stories throughout Australia and internationally.

Through Travel Radio Australia, SKI TV and Diamond Film Productions, the company works with tourism boards, airlines, ski resorts, event organisers and production partners to create engaging multimedia content.

OUR JOURNEY

Nearly Five Decades of Media Experience

From commercial radio to international broadcasting, travel media, television and film production.

1976

Commercial Radio Begins

Geoff Harrison commenced a career in Australian commercial radio, developing experience in announcing, journalism and broadcasting.

1988

Calgary Winter Olympics

Accredited media coverage of the XV Olympic Winter Games, reporting internationally on alpine sport and tourism.

1994

Lillehammer Winter Olympics

International broadcasting and journalism covering one of the world's most recognised winter sporting events.

2024

Expansion of Australia's travel broadcasting platform featuring destination interviews, tourism news and travel storytelling.
