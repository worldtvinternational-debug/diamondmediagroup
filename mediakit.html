COMPANY

About Diamond Media Group

Diamond Media Group is an Australian media and entertainment company producing premium content across travel broadcasting, digital television, feature film development, radio programming and live entertainment.

The company operates a growing portfolio of specialist media brands serving Australian and international audiences through broadcast, streaming, digital publishing, podcasting, video production and event coverage.

Diamond Media Group's current portfolio includes Travel Radio Australia, SKI TV, Diamond Film Productions and a growing range of multimedia projects designed to connect audiences with travel, tourism, aviation, entertainment and lifestyle experiences.

FOUNDER

Executive Profile

Geoffrey Harrison

Founder & Managing Director

Geoffrey Harrison is an Australian broadcaster, television producer, journalist and media entrepreneur with more than four decades of experience in radio, television, film production and digital media.

His career has included commercial radio, news broadcasting, tourism promotion, international television production, major event coverage and feature film development.

Today he leads Diamond Media Group and oversees the company's expanding portfolio including Travel Radio Australia, SKI TV and Diamond Film Productions.
