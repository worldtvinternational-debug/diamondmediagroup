PRESS RELEASE August 2026

Diamond Media Group and Travel Radio Australia to Attend MIPCOM CANNES 2026

Australian media and entertainment company returns to the world's leading global content market in Cannes.

MIPCOM CANNES 2026
12–15 October 2026
Palais des Festivals, Cannes, France

Diamond Media Group has announced that it will attend MIPCOM CANNES 2026 in Cannes, France, from 12–15 October, with its international media platform Travel Radio Australia represented at one of the world's most important gatherings for the global television, film and digital content industries.

Travel Radio Australia's presenter and producer Geoff Harrison will attend MIPCOM CANNES for the fifth consecutive year as an accredited member of the international press.

Held annually at the Palais des Festivals in Cannes, MIPCOM brings together television networks, streaming platforms, studios, production companies, distributors, creators, brands, technology companies and buyers from around the world.

Diamond Media Group will use its presence at MIPCOM to develop international relationships across its growing portfolio of media, entertainment and digital projects, while Travel Radio Australia will continue building its international network of broadcasters, tourism organisations, production companies, travel brands and media partners.

Harrison's fifth consecutive MIPCOM attendance also provides Travel Radio Australia with the opportunity to report directly from one of the world's major international media markets.

"Returning to MIPCOM for the fifth consecutive year is a significant milestone for Travel Radio Australia. MIPCOM is one of the few places in the world where you can meet such a concentrated group of international broadcasters, producers, distributors, streaming companies and content creators."

Geoff Harrison, Presenter & Producer, Travel Radio Australia

The 2026 MIPCOM programme will examine major changes affecting the international content industry, including artificial intelligence, creator-led entertainment, digital distribution, branded content and emerging formats.

About Diamond Media Group

Diamond Media Group is an Australian media and entertainment company involved in film, television, digital broadcasting, travel media and entertainment production.

About Travel Radio Australia

Travel Radio Australia is an international travel broadcasting and digital media platform featuring travel destinations, tourism, aviation, hospitality, adventure and the people shaping the global travel industry.

Diamond Media Group Launches Travel Radio Australia Interview Archive

Diamond Media Group has launched a new Travel Radio Australia Interview Archive, providing listeners, travel professionals and industry audiences with easy access to a growing collection of interviews from Australia and around the world.

The archive allows visitors to search interviews, listen online, access transcripts and gives interviewees the ability to download, share and embed their interviews on their own websites and social media platforms.
