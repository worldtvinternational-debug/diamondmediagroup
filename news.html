PRESS RELEASE October 2026

Rudolf Buitendach Officially Attached as Director of The Pensioner Gang Movie

Feature film based on true events, with a novel published on Amazon, advances towards planned production in London and Amsterdam in April/May 2027.

THE PENSIONER GANG MOVIE
Director: Rudolf Buitendach
Writer & Producer: Geoffrey Harrison
Diamond Film Productions / Diamond Media Group
Planned production: April/May 2027
Proposed budget: Up to €5 million

Diamond Media Group announces that internationally experienced filmmaker Rudolf Buitendach has officially attached as director of The Pensioner Gang Movie, a feature film based on true events and developed from the published novel by Geoffrey Harrison.

Set in London and Amsterdam in 2008, the crime drama/thriller is progressing through pre-production, with principal photography planned for April/May 2027. Financing, co-production and distribution discussions form part of the project's development strategy.

An International Feature-Film Director

Rudolf Buitendach is a South African-born director, editor, writer and producer whose credits include Dark Hearts (2014), Where the Road Runs Out (2014), Apartment 407 (2016) and Hex (2018).

His wider experience includes feature-film production, international post-production, theatrical trailers, the Netflix feature Angeliena (2021) and the documentary Medicine of the Wolf (2015). His background combines creative direction with substantial film editing and post-production experience.

Writer and Producer Welcomes Appointment

"I am absolutely thrilled that Rudolf has officially attached as director for The Pensioner Gang Movie. His vast experience in directing and producing both feature films and TV series will be invaluable for The Pensioner Gang Movie."

Geoff Harrison, Writer & Producer

Moving Towards Production

The proposed production budget is capped at €5 million, with filming planned in London and Amsterdam in April/May 2027. The production welcomes enquiries from potential investors, co-production partners, distributors and other film-industry professionals.

About The Pensioner Gang Movie

The Pensioner Gang Movie is written and produced by Geoffrey Harrison. Based on true events, the story has also been published as a novel available through Amazon. The planned film is set in 2008.

PRESS RELEASE September 2026

The Pensioner Gang Now a Published Novel on Amazon Kindle

Diamond Media Group announces the publication of Geoffrey Harrison's crime drama novel ahead of the feature film's international development.

THE PENSIONER GANG
Published September 2026
Amazon Kindle
ASIN: B0HJP11ZML

Diamond Media Group has announced that The Pensioner Gang, written by Australian broadcaster and producer Geoffrey Harrison, is now a published novel available on Amazon Kindle.

The Pensioner Gang: They thought they were done. Crime Didn't Agree. is a crime drama combining humour, suspense, international intrigue and larger-than-life characters.

Set against London and Amsterdam in 2008, the story follows a group of retired criminals whose past catches up with them when they become involved in one final, high-stakes operation.

What begins as an unlikely reunion develops into a story involving organised crime, betrayal, international connections, financial intrigue and a missing £60 million.

From Novel to Feature Film

The publication of the novel represents an important milestone in Diamond Media Group's development of The Pensioner Gang as an international feature film project.

The feature film is currently in development, with the project being developed for international audiences and production planning focused on London and Amsterdam.

The published novel now provides audiences with the opportunity to discover the story and its characters while the feature film continues through development.

"Publishing the novel is a major milestone for The Pensioner Gang. It means the story now exists as a completed published work while we continue developing the feature film. The novel allows audiences to discover the characters and story now, while the film project takes the next step toward production."

Geoff Harrison, Author & Producer

Now Available on Amazon Kindle

The Pensioner Gang is available now as a Kindle eBook through Amazon.

PRESS RELEASE August 2026

Diamond Media Group and Travel Radio Australia to Attend MIPCOM CANNES 2026

Australian media and entertainment company returns to the world's leading global content market in Cannes.

MIPCOM CANNES 2026
12–15 October 2026
Palais des Festivals, Cannes, France

Diamond Media Group has announced that it will attend MIPCOM CANNES 2026 in Cannes, France, from 12–15 October, with its international media platform Travel Radio Australia represented at one of the world's most important gatherings for the global television, film and digital content industries.

Travel Radio Australia's presenter and producer Geoff Harrison will attend MIPCOM CANNES for the fifth consecutive year as an accredited member of the international press.

Held annually at the Palais des Festivals in Cannes, MIPCOM brings together television networks, streaming platforms, studios, production companies, distributors, creators, brands, technology companies and buyers from around the world.

Diamond Media Group will use its presence at MIPCOM to develop international relationships across its growing portfolio of media, entertainment and digital projects, while Travel Radio Australia continues building its international network of broadcasters, tourism organisations, production companies, travel brands and media partners.

Harrison's fifth consecutive MIPCOM attendance also provides Travel Radio Australia with the opportunity to report directly from one of the world's major international media markets.

"Returning to MIPCOM for the fifth consecutive year is a significant milestone for Travel Radio Australia. MIPCOM is one of the few places in the world where you can meet such a concentrated group of international broadcasters, producers, distributors, streaming companies and content creators."

Geoff Harrison, Presenter & Producer, Travel Radio Australia

The 2026 MIPCOM programme will examine major changes affecting the international content industry, including artificial intelligence, creator-led entertainment, digital distribution, branded content and emerging formats.

About Travel Radio Australia

Travel Radio Australia is an international travel broadcasting and digital media platform featuring travel destinations, tourism, aviation, hospitality, adventure and the people shaping the global travel industry.
