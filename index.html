ABOUT DIAMOND MEDIA GROUP

Australia's Independent Media & Entertainment Network

Diamond Media Group is an Australian independent media company producing premium travel broadcasting, television, feature films, digital publishing and live entertainment for audiences across Australia and around the world.

With nearly five decades of broadcasting and media experience, Diamond Media Group brings together journalism, television production, radio, film and digital storytelling under one internationally focused media brand.

FEATURED PRODUCTION

The Pensioner Gang Movie

In Development

Inspired by true events, The Pensioner Gang Movie follows a group of retired criminals who reunite for one final, high-stakes mission. Blending crime, humour and drama, the film is being developed by Diamond Film Studio for an international audience.

Development includes screenplay refinement, investment discussions, location planning and international production partnerships.
